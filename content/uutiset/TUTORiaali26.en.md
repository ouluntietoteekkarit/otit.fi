Welcome to TUTORial, OTiT’s very first event of the freshman year.

The event starts at 14:00 near the engineer sphere located in the inner courtyard of Oulu University of Applied Sciences (OAMK), from where we will head together to the engineer lawn on the east side of the AMK building. The exact location will be shared in the OTiT 2026-group! At the event, you’ll get to know other new students as well as senior students. There will also be some light snacks and casual hanging out.

If you have any questions regarding the event, feel free to send a message on Telegram to either the Minister of Freshman Affairs (@keppana3) or the Secretary of Freshman Affairs (@Kor1pallo).
